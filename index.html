    A fresh 10-minute round as the same player: 0 happiness, full energy, 10 poop balls, an empty bladder, full fountains, fresh items, cleared discoveries and no dropped balls or puddles. Saved leaderboard scores stay.

    Little joys, big adventures

    Hold left-click near an item for the time shown in its prompt. Time grows with energy and happiness rewards, with a one-second minimum. Finish the whole hold to earn its rewards. Carrots give 10 happiness, apples 50, rabbit food 80, bananas 40, bread 20, noodles 10, cake 150 and books 20. Cardboard gives 100, wires 150 and slippers 200.

    Wires return to their original place after 30 seconds, even if pushed away. Up to eight boxes return in new places after 60 seconds; up to four pairs of slippers return after 90. Cake returns to the kitchen dining table after 120 seconds, even if pushed away, with at most one available. When fewer than two other snacks remain, one random regular snack appears every minute. Menus pause all timers.

    Shift and charged leaps use energy. Walking footsteps are silent; sprinting makes footstep sounds and local noise. Energy recovers by 0.01% every half second. Every charged jump launched inside the den is free, including stationary jumps and repeat escapes. The special hay in your den restores energy to 100% after a four-second hold, but gives no happiness. Press R to return home while preserving supplies, time, score and the current round. Walk into consumables to push them with a slight slowdown. They slide and can fall off furniture; the special hay stays inside the den. Loose items move instead of serving as steps. Tables are not hiding spots: once the mistress stands within one metre of a table on the same floor, she can catch Shidan or clean messes across the whole tabletop, including the far side, or underneath. Walls, table legs and closed glass still block her reach. Beds, sofas, the island and the greenhouse roof are also catchable surfaces. The low greenhouse tunnel remains safe. The former library hiding passage is now the single staircase into the basement. Its cement floor has firm grip, with pillars, a furnace, a water heater and ceiling lamps; food and fountains do not spawn there. Upstairs, a ramp leads to two shelf levels with items; both levels are within the mistress's reach. Use the grippy upper shelf for charged jumps toward the desk or across the studio.

    Shidan starts with 10 poop balls and can carry 20. Right-click or P uses one, with a one-second wait between balls. Carrots, books, apples, rabbit food, bananas and bread refill two; hay refills three; noodles and cake refill five. Special den hay refills three once per visit: leave and re-enter the den to earn more. Its energy refill remains available. Other items give no balls. Eat a dropped ball to recover five energy. Up to 1,000 dropped balls stay until eaten or cleaned. Visible poop distracts the mistress: she runs over and picks it up in 0.2 seconds. Completed pee puddles take priority and occupy her for fifteen seconds. Every cleaned puddle adds 5% of your top sprint speed to all her movement for the rest of the round; bonuses stack. After cleanup she checks remembered chewing, otherwise chases you if visible or starts searching.

    The rooms above ground and both yards have a fixed water fountain holding enough water to fill one whole bladder. Hold left-click nearby to drink at 10% per second. Shidan makes a little nibbling motion. Partial drinks leave the unused water for later; an empty fountain stays empty until a new round or Restart game. Once her bladder reaches 100%, press Q to pee for one second, gradually emptying her bladder into a puddle. Movement, jumping, eating, drinking and pooping wait until she finishes. She cannot be caught during this full one-second action; the mistress can start cleaning its puddle once it is complete. A 60-second cooldown begins when peeing starts; the larger visible timers show when peeing and drinking are available again.

    The roof and ceilings stay visible while playing. Use V or a house tour to inspect the rooms with the roof and ceilings hidden. Show roof closes the house; Look inside opens it again.

    Your day lasts ten minutes, reaching full night with two minutes left. At bedtime your happiness score is saved under your name in this browser. Game Over shows your final score, local rank and round statistics, with options for a new game or the main menu. The mistress follows household routines. Her red minimap dot appears only while she is in view. Nearby walking creates fading red rings on the minimap within five metres, even through walls. Listen for softer search steps, steady routine footsteps and quicker, firmer running steps; they grow quieter with distance. Sound starts on and can be muted with the Sound button. Small red ripples follow her feet, with more during a chase. Her amber floor pattern shows a 90-degree forward view reaching fifty metres. The same sight works during chores and searching: visible parts of Shidan's body, head or ears can give her away, including on furniture. She rechecks promptly when she turns or Shidan climbs onto a table. Red warns of pursuit; the chase and search warning also shows her current distance from Shidan in metres. Blue means her own noise masks hearing, but she can still see. Break sight for more than two seconds to make her search, or use a low passage. If she only hears you, she runs to that sound before searching. Each noisy activity creates a random 1–5 second cover window, followed by a quiet gap. The blue noise-cover badge stays visible while she is actually making noise; leaving the activity ends the cover. She can still see you. Happiness-granting chewing can be heard from both stairs, any floor, the balcony and both yards whenever she is quiet, except from the protected den or hiding passages. Sprint noise has a two-second grace period after she slows. If caught, she bends down, reaches for Shidan and smoothly lifts her into her arms over three seconds before returning her to the den. She then appears beside the den to scold you for five seconds before leaving for a random activity. Her field of view stays active during the rant; a visible escape or fresh happiness-granting chewing immediately starts another chase when she is quiet. Her fifteen routines visit every room, the balcony and both yards. She can pursue outside, starting at 60% of Shidan’s sprint speed and gaining five percentage points every two minutes, up to 80%.

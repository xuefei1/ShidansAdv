    Little joys, big adventures

    Hold left-click near a snack for one full second to eat. Shift uses energy for sprinting; charged leaps cost 5–10 energy. Ordinary movement and tap hops are free.

    The special hay inside your den always refills energy to 100% after a one-second hold. It never runs out. Energy also slowly recovers on its own. Snacks restore energy over one second. When fewer than two remain, one new snack appears every minute. Menus pause these timers; returning home preserves supplies.

    Hold Space for a longer leap on bedding, carpet, or outdoors. Indoor wood permits only a small hop. The mistress, happiness scores, and bedtime are coming later.
